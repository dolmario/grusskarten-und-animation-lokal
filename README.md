# AI greeting cards and animated photos with ComfyUI, locally

Two beginner tutorials: make a greeting card image, then bring a photo or card to life with LTX-2.5. Everything runs on your own computer, no cloud.

| Task | Download | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Greeting cards | [01-grusskarten-v2.zip](downloads/01-grusskarten-v2.zip) | [English](01-grusskarten/START-EN.md) · [Deutsch](01-grusskarten/START-DE.md) |
| 2. Animate an image | [02-animation-v2.zip](downloads/02-animation-v2.zip) | [English](02-animation/START-EN.md) · [Deutsch](02-animation/START-DE.md) |

1. Open the ZIP link and click **Download raw file**.
2. Extract the ZIP completely and open `START-EN.md`.
3. Import the `.ui.json` workflow into ComfyUI. Download the models from the official links in the guide.

The ZIPs contain workflows, guide, prompts and a checklist; the animation kit also contains an example photo from the Library of Congress (no known restrictions on publication, see [sources and rights](02-animation/SOURCES-AND-RIGHTS.md)).
